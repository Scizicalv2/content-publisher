Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Content Publisher is a private tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to manage the YouTube channel the owner chose: uploading videos, setting thumbnails, editing video details, posting comments and reading that channel's statistics. It does not access any other Google data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Content Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it makes the stored tokens stop working.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Scizicalv2/content-publisher.
